# Creative AI Bootcamp — working plan

## Page concept

The reference [NextLeap Applied Generative AI Bootcamp](https://nextleap.app/lp/course/generative-ai-course) makes the offer legible quickly: a clear promise, duration and workload, a week-by-week curriculum, projects, teaching format, tools, requirements, and a repeated conversion action. The new `/creative-bootcamp` page uses that information flow while following VamshiCreates' own portrait-led split hero, floating navigation, blue calls to action, restrained cards, and editorial type.

This is a **proposed program**. Cohort dates, live session times, price, admissions policy, certificate, and testimonials are intentionally absent until confirmed.

## Who it is for

Early-career designers, creators, editors, and marketers who want to turn briefs into coherent campaign work across static design, 3D, video, and AI-assisted workflows. Prior design experience is helpful but not required; routine computer literacy and weekly practice are required.

## Program shape

- **Length:** 10 weeks, proposed.
- **Commitment:** 8–10 hours per week, proposed.
- **Teaching rhythm:** One concept workshop, one guided build and critique, and independent work each week. Exact live days and times remain to be set.
- **Outcome:** Six portfolio outputs culminating in one cohesive campaign and a process case study.
- **Feedback:** Brief-driven exercises, live critique, revision, and a final presentation.

| Week | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Design principles and Figma | Creative direction board |
| 2 | Photoshop and image making | Campaign key visual |
| 3 | Illustrator and brand identity | Mini identity system |
| 4 | Campaign system across formats | Multi-format campaign kit |
| 5 | Blender foundations | 3D product render |
| 6 | 3D motion and compositing | Animated brand scene |
| 7 | Editing, sound, and story | 30-second film |
| 8 | Directed AI video | AI-assisted sequence |
| 9 | LLMs and MCP workflows | Documented creative workflow |
| 10 | Production, critique, and presentation | Portfolio capstone |

The website groups intermediate weekly work into **six portfolio projects**: campaign key visual, identity system, 3D scene, short film, creative workflow, and capstone.

## Entry setup

- **Computer:** At least 16 GB RAM; recommend 32 GB RAM, fast SSD storage, and a capable GPU for heavier Blender and video work. This is a program admission threshold, not a claim that every application requires 16 GB. Blender's [recommended memory and GPU](https://www.blender.org/download/requirements/) are 32 GB RAM and 8 GB VRAM. Adobe recommends [16 GB or more for Photoshop](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements-installation/adobe-photoshop-on-desktop-technical-requirements.html) and [32 GB or more for 4K Premiere work](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/adobe-premiere-pro-technical-requirements.html).
- **Software:** Access to Photoshop and Illustrator; Figma, Blender, and DaVinci Resolve in the teaching workflow. Confirm the exact software versions and licensing before collecting payment.
- **AI:** Entry-level paid access to the chosen AI path, pending the final policy on whether learners need one or all three. Google Antigravity is an agent workspace; its paid individual path is a [Google AI plan](https://antigravity.google/pricing). ChatGPT and Claude have their own [paid plans](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) and [plan details](https://www.anthropic.com/pricing).
- **Other:** Reliable internet, headphones, and room for large project files. Some AI video services may have separate usage limits or costs; specify those before enrollment.
